⭐ Starching & Finishing Standards

Choose the exact stiffness and sheen that matches your preference and occasion.

⭐ Starch Only

Natural, comfortable firm touch. Ideal for daily casual wear, light work days, and people who prefer a softer, flexible fit on the head.

🛡️ Tanganran Finish

The time-honored traditional rock-hard stiffness. Your cap maintains an immovable structure and razor-sharp crease through full days of events.

✨ Glass Finish

Our signature royal treatment. Combines unmatched stiffness with a smooth, glossy protective sheen that repels moisture and gives your cap a radiant luster.
